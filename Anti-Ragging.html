Anti Ragging

We are committed to supporting our students in achieving their academic goals, regardless of their financial background.

Ragging is a punishable offense as per the directions of Hon'ble Supreme Court of India. Strict action will be taken against those found guilty of ragging.

Report Ragging
What is Ragging?

Ragging is any disorderly conduct, whether by words spoken or written, or by an act which has the effect of teasing, treating, or handling with rudeness any student, indulging in rowdy or undisciplined activities which cause or are likely to cause annoyance, hardship or psychological harm, or to raise fear or apprehension thereof in a fresher or a junior student.

Ragging also includes asking the students to do any act or perform something which such a student will not do in the ordinary course and which has the effect of causing or generating a sense of shame or embarrassment so as to adversely affect the physique or psyche of a fresher or a junior student.

Forms of Ragging

Physical Abuse

Causing hurt, harm, or injury to another student. This includes physical assault of any nature.

Verbal Abuse

Teasing, taunting, or insulting another student, particularly targeting their appearance, religion, caste, or economic background.

Forced Activity

Compelling a student to do something they would not willingly do, such as singing, dancing, or performing other acts.

Financial Exploitation

Forcing students to spend money on treats, parties, or other expenses.

Psychological Pressure

Creating fear, intimidation, or mental stress that affects a student's well-being.

Punishments for Ragging

As per UGC regulations and Supreme Court directives

Institutional Level Punishments
  1. Suspension from attending classes and academic privileges
  2. Withholding/withdrawing scholarship/fellowship and other benefits
  3. Debarring from appearing in any test/examination or other evaluation process 
  4. Withholding results 
  5. Expulsion from the hostel/institution 
  6. Cancellation of admission 
  7. Rustication from the institution for periods ranging from one to four semesters
Legal Punishments
  1. First Information Report (FIR) filed with the police
  2. Imprisonment up to 2 years as per the Anti-Ragging Act
  3. Fine up to Rs. 25,000
  4. Criminal proceedings under the Indian Penal Court
  5. Collective punishment if the individuals committing or abetting ragging are not identified

Preventive Measures

Steps taken by the institution to prevent ragging

Awareness Programs

Regular seminars, workshops, and orientation programs to create awareness about the consequences of ragging among students.

Monitoring System

Active surveillance in hostels, common areas, and other locations through regular inspections by staff and security personnel.

Anti-Ragging Squad

Dedicated squad that conducts surprise checks and maintains vigilance to prevent ragging incidents.

Anti-Ragging Committee

Committee responsible for implementing anti-ragging measures and addressing complaints promptly.

Anti-Ragging Undertaking

Mandatory submission of anti-ragging undertaking by students and parents at the time of admission.

Complaint Mechanism

Multiple channels for reporting ragging incidents, ensuring confidentiality and prompt action.
