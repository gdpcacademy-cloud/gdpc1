Admission Process

Embark on an exciting academic journey with us by following our simple admission process.

Eligibility Criteria

Before applying, ensure you meet the following requirements:

Educational Qualification

Completion of 10th Grade with a minimum of 45% marks.

Age Criteria

Applicants must be at least 15 years old.

Entrance Exam

Candidates must qualify B.P.T.P.I.A entrance exam conducted by Bihar Private Technical & Professional Institutions Association.

Required Documents

Ensure you have the following documents ready

Mark sheet of 10th Grade

Original and photocopy required

Transfer Certificate (TC)

TC and/or ​School Leaving Certificate (SLC)

Domicile Certificate

Domicile Certificate and/or Caste Certificate (if applicable)

Passport-sized Photographs

6 Recent photographs

Entrance Exam Scorecard

B.P.T.P.I.A exam result

ID Proof

Aadhar Card and/or other valid ID

How to Apply

Before applying, ensure you meet the following requirements:

GDPC Admission Process 
  1. Collect the application form from the college office
  2. Fill it out completely
  3. Submit along with required documents to the admission office

Admission Timeline

Stay on track with our admission schedule

Merit List: May - June
Second Session
Application Period: July - August
Last Date for Submission: 31st August
Entrance Exam: September

Selection Process

Admissions are granted based on:

  • Academic performance and eligibility
  • Merit list or entrance exam scores
  • Counselling session and documents verification
