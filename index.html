Welcome to Girija Devi Polytechnic College (GDPC)

Founded on 31st May 2015, Girija Devi Polytechnic College operates under the Suchit Singh Memorial Educational Trust and is named after the revered Girija Devi. We are committed to providing high-quality technical education to aspiring students in Bihar.

Our institution is committed to academic excellence, practical training, and holistic development of students to prepare them for successful careers in the industry.

Our Vision

To empower young minds through quality technical education, practical skills, and career-focused learning, creating confident and capable professionals for the future.

Lateral Entry Programme

Already completed Class 12th (PCM) or ITI? Take the next step toward your engineering career!

Lateral Entry 
Program

Lateral Entry Programme offers eligible students an opportunity to pursue Diploma Engineering through direct admission to an advanced level. If you have completed Class 12th with Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics (PCM) or ITI, this pathway can help you continue your technical education, strengthen your practical skills, and move closer to your career goals.

With a focus on technical knowledge, practical learning, and career development, GDPC provides the right environment to turn your existing qualifications into valuable engineering skills.
